Course Quality & Support

Report a Course Issue

Noticed a broken resource, confusing direction, or something in a CVA course that needs attention? Let our course development team know. Your report helps keep learning materials accurate, accessible, and ready for students.

When to submit a request

What Should I Report?

  • Broken links or resources: A video, document, interactive, or resource is missing or does not open.
  • Content corrections: Directions, explanations, or other course material contain an error.
  • Assessments and answer keys: A question, answer, rubric, or scoring guidance needs review.
  • Accessibility concerns: Students may have difficulty accessing required learning materials.
  • Technical or CTLS issues: A course component is not functioning correctly.
  • Instructional improvements: A suggestion could make learning clearer or more effective.

Student work affected right now? Continue supporting the student through normal CVA communication and intervention procedures while the request is reviewed. This report does not itself resolve an immediate access barrier.

Three quick steps

How to Submit a Request

1

Choose the Course

Select the CVA course from the form's dropdown.

2

Describe the Issue

Identify the unit, lesson, assignment, or page and explain what needs attention.

3

Explain the Impact

Tell us how the issue affects students so the team can determine its priority.

You do not need to provide a CTLS page link. The course name and specific location are enough to begin investigating.

After you submit

What Happens Next?

Your request enters the CVA course maintenance process. The team reviews the report, determines next steps, and assigns work as appropriate.

You should receive a Microsoft Teams acknowledgement after a successful submission and a resolution notification when the issue has been reviewed, verified, and closed.

Keep in mind: Use this form to report issues in CVA-maintained course content. For student-specific concerns, grading questions, or daily classroom support, continue using the appropriate CVA procedures.

Common questions

Before You Submit

Do I need to include a link?
No. CTLS pages may not have a stable shareable link. Provide the course and unit, lesson, assignment, or page title.
Can I suggest an improvement?
Yes. Share instructional improvements as well as corrections and technical issues. Explain how the change could help students.
What if a student cannot complete required work?
Indicate that impact in the form. Keep communicating with and supporting the student while the issue is reviewed.
