Student Support

Student Accommodations in a CVA Class

Some students in your CVA class may have approved accommodations through an IEP or 504 Plan. As a CVA adjunct teacher, your role is to review, understand, and implement the approved accommodations shared with you.

Overview

Know Where to Look and Who to Contact

Accommodations help ensure students have access to the supports they are approved to receive. In CVA, you may learn about a student’s accommodations in more than one way, and CVA Teacher Support will help coordinate information when needed.

Important: Accommodations in CVA may look different from a face-to-face classroom. Review the details provided and reach out to CVA Teacher Support with questions or concerns.

How You’ll Know

How You May Learn a Student Has IEP or 504 Plan Accommodations

There are several ways you may become aware that a student has approved accommodations.

Source 1

Student Self-Report

A student may self-report accommodations through Flex Ed or by contacting you directly. Always verify that information is accurate by checking Student Accommodations in Synergy.

Source 2

Synergy

You may access student information in Synergy to review details connected to the student’s plan.

Source 3

CVA Student Support

CVA Student Support will email accommodation details when information needs to be shared with you.

Your Role

Your Role as a CVA Adjunct Teacher

Once you are aware of a student’s approved accommodations, use the guidance provided to support the student appropriately in the CVA learning environment.

Step 1

Review and Implement Approved Accommodations

Review the accommodation information provided and implement the approved accommodations. Remember that CVA accommodations may be different from accommodations used in a face-to-face classroom.

Step 2

Case Managers and Outside Agencies

Do not respond to Case Manager requests for progress updates or any questions from outside agencies. Instead, direct them to CVA Student Support for assistance and notify CVA Teacher Support.

Step 3

Ask Questions Early

Send questions or concerns to CVA Teacher Support so CVA Teacher Support can help coordinate next steps.

Access

Viewing 504 Plans

All teachers have access to their respective students’ 504 Plans of Accommodations via Synergy Gradebook.

Step 1

Synergy Seating Chart

Once signed in to your Synergy Seating Chart, reference the email sent to you separately (each term) about which students are currently served through a 504 plan.

Step 2

Finding the 504 Plan

Once you have identified a specific student with a 504 plan, click on that strong student’s picture and select 504 Plan from the the pop-up menu. The student’s 504 Plan of Accommodations will then open up as a PDF.

Step 3

Accommodation Types

Scroll down to the Accommodations Types section to view that student’s specific accommodations.

Access

Viewing IEP Plans

Synergy Gradebook gives teachers immediate access to all of the accommodations for any student in their courses who is served through Special Education.

Step 1

Finding the Students

Once signed in to your Synergy Gradebook, hover your cursor over the Home tab at the top.

Step 2

Student Accommodations

From the menu that drops down, select Student Accommodations. All students who are served through Special Education will appear in a list on the left-hand side of the screen.

Step 3

Viewing the accommodations

To access each student’s specific accommodations, simply click on a student’s picture and the list of accommodations from that student’s last finalized IEP will appear.

Accommodations

Universal Accommodations in Online Learning

Students with an IEP or 504 Plan can still receive accommodations and support in CVA courses. Because learning looks a little different in an online environment, not every accommodation may apply in the same way. Our goal is to provide the supports that can be reasonably implemented in the virtual setting so students can access the course and be successful.

Agenda Click to learn more

Class schedule with due dates is located in course information. It includes information for the entire semester and should be printed out to assist students in keeping track of due dates, completion of coursework, and grades.

Extended Time: Tests Click to learn more

A timer may be visible during quizzes and tests. It does not shut off when time lapses on the quiz or test, but it does allow the teacher to track the amount of time a student uses for a test. Students can simply continue with the quiz or test if additional time is needed.

Extended Time: Assignments & Projects Click to learn more

Students have 24/7 access to class instruction, assignments, and assessments upon enrollment with CVA. The Class Schedule allows advance planning for assignment completion as all due dates are outlined at the beginning of the term. Students have the flexibility to begin an assignment earlier, if needed, in order to spend additional time. Students who have health issues that periodically impact their ability to complete schoolwork due to illness have the flexibility to work ahead when they are feeling well. Note: After the due date lapses, teachers will mark the item “missing” and a question mark with a placeholder score of 0% will appear in the gradebook. When the student submits the assignment and it is graded, the teacher will replace the 0% with the score earned. There is no academic penalty for work submitted past the due date. However, the mid-point and final deadlines for submitting all classwork is firm and is posted on each Class Schedule.

Copies of Notes & Study Guides Click to learn more

Course materials may be printed out and reviewed or highlighted as needed.

Monitoring of Student Progress Click to learn more

Student and parent/guardian observers have access to Student’s Blackboard grade book and are encouraged to check progress frequently against course schedule due dates.

Use of word processor/computer Click to learn more

Required to complete online course. Students complete assignments using a computer and upload them directly into the course.

Read to/Speak Click to learn more

Read aloud application available for many lessons and tests.

Small group testing/Preferential seating Click to learn more

All students work individually on a computer.

Marking on tests Click to learn more

All assessment answers are entered online.

Student support Click to learn more

CVA Learning Center is available for additional student support. Check CVA web site for days and hours.

Reduction in number of problems in mathematics Click to learn more

All practice assignments and mastery quizzes have only 10 items; practice assignment items are scaffolded to ensure mastery of concepts.

Rephrasing/clarifying/chunking assignments Click to learn more

Students should contact their instructor(s) with specific questions and can move to the next lesson while waiting for a response. Instructors will reply within 24-48 hours.

Read aloud for assessments Click to learn more

Any adult can read a test aloud for a student, and the CVA Learning Center is open 9:00 am- 5:00 pm daily if a student requires additional assistance.

Accessibility Tools

Built-In Supports in CTLS Student

Students can locate Accessibility Tools in the top-right corner of CTLS Student. These tools can support reading, viewing, listening, and reviewing course content.

Teacher Tip: These tools may support all students, not only students with formal accommodations. Encourage students to use the tools that help them access and understand course content.

Line Reader Click to learn more

Helps students focus on one line of text at a time by blocking out the rest of the screen. Best for when text feels overwhelming or hard to follow.

Color Contrast Click to learn more

Lets students change how text and background colors appear. Best for when text is difficult to see or causes eye fatigue.

Read Aloud Click to learn more

Reads text aloud while students follow along. Best for when students need help understanding complex text or prefer listening.

Captions Click to learn more

Displays spoken words as text during a video. Best for when audio is unclear or students want to read and listen at the same time.

Transcripts Click to learn more

Provides a full written version of everything said in a video. Best for studying, reviewing, or looking for specific information.

Audio Descriptions Click to learn more

Audio descriptions provide spoken explanations of important visual details in a video, including actions, expressions, settings, and on-screen text not covered by dialogue.

Quick Reference

What Should I Do?

If a student self-reports...

Review available information in Synergy and contact CVA Teacher Support if you need clarification.

If you receive accommodation details...

Review the approved accommodations and implement them in your CVA class.

If a Case Manager requests updates...

Do not respond directly to the local schools or any other agency. Forward those questions to CVA Teacher Support.

Important Reminder

When in Doubt, Contact CVA Teacher Support

Do not guess about how to interpret or implement accommodations. If you have questions, concerns, or need clarification, contact cvateachersupport@cobbk12.org.
