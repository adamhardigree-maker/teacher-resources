Adding your announcement to CTLS
In your CVA classroom, select the </> HTML/source editor and paste the copied code. Always check the announcement’s appearance after saving.
Want to add a picture?
After pasting your announcement HTML, use the Rich Text editor in CTLS to upload and insert an image. Be sure to add ALT text that briefly describes meaningful information in the picture so students using screen readers can understand it.
